FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you get started.

The Free Plan allows you to create up to 3 tasks. Once the maximum is reached, additional tasks require Pro access.

TaskPilot is designed to support task planning and management. Availability of offline functionality may depend on the app version, device, and service configuration.

Pro costs ₹399 for 30 days and provides additional functionality, including unlimited task creation and the ability to reuse or repeat previously created tasks across selected dates or periods using available timeline/repeat functionality.

You retain ownership of task content you create. TaskPilot processes and stores task information to provide task-management and task functionality. Task content is not sold for advertising purposes and is not publicly displayed through the Service.

Pro subscriptions currently do not automatically renew. Pro access remains available for 30 days from the applicable purchase or activation period. After expiry, another purchase is required to continue using Pro features.
