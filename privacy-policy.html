Task Alert — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Task Alert ("Task Alert", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using our task-management and productivity service.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

When you create and use a Task Alert account, we may collect and store:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Password information
  • Email verification information

Your password is stored using appropriate password-hashing/security mechanisms and is not intended to be stored as plain-text password data.

Task and Reminder Information

We collect and store information that you create within Task Alert, including task names or descriptions, scheduled dates, scheduled times, reminder information, task status, timeline/repeat information, and other information necessary to provide task-management functionality.

You retain ownership of the content you create.

Device and Notification Information

To provide reminder notifications, we may collect and store a device or push-notification token associated with your account or device.

This allows Task Alert to send relevant notifications to your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Authenticate your account.
  • Verify your email address.
  • Provide login and password-recovery functionality.
  • Store and display your tasks and reminders.
  • Schedule and deliver notifications.
  • Provide Free and Pro functionality.
  • Process subscription payments.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Investigate and resolve technical issues.
  • Maintain and secure the Service.
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized or abusive activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or task content for advertising purposes.

3. How Your Task Data Is Used

Your task and reminder information is primarily processed and stored so that Task Alert can provide the functionality you request.

For example, we need to store your tasks so that you can access them later through your account and so that scheduled reminders can be generated.

Your tasks are not intended to be publicly displayed or made available to other Task Alert users.

4. Account Authentication and Security

Task Alert uses authentication mechanisms to protect your account.

Passwords are handled using password-hashing/security practices rather than being stored as readable plain-text passwords.

You are responsible for keeping your email address and password secure.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. While we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, security incidents, or other failures will never occur.

5. Notifications

Task Alert may use push-notification services to deliver reminders to your device.

To receive notifications, you must provide the necessary notification permission on your device.

Notifications may include information necessary to identify or remind you about a scheduled task.

Notification delivery can be affected by device settings, operating-system restrictions, network conditions, third-party notification infrastructure, maintenance, or other technical circumstances.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use third-party providers to help operate Task Alert.

These may include services for:

  • Payment processing: Razorpay
  • Push notifications: Expo and/or Firebase
  • Email delivery: Resend and/or Gmail
  • Hosting/server infrastructure: applicable hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Other technical services required to operate and maintain Task Alert

These providers may process information on our behalf where necessary to provide their services.

We do not sell your personal information to these providers for advertising purposes.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and terms governing their services.

7. Payments

When you purchase Task Alert Pro, payment processing is handled through Razorpay or another payment provider made available by Task Alert.

Payment providers may collect information required to process your transaction.

Task Alert does not need to store your complete payment-card information in order to provide the subscription service.

Payment information may also be retained by the applicable payment provider according to its own policies and legal obligations.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or task content.

We may disclose information only where reasonably necessary, including:

  • To service providers that help us operate Task Alert.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Service.
  • To investigate fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity.
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, court orders, or valid requests from authorized government or law-enforcement authorities.
  • Where disclosure is otherwise necessary or permitted by applicable law.

Where legally permitted, we may take reasonable steps to limit disclosures to information relevant to the applicable request or purpose.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account and task information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service and maintain your account.

If an account is terminated, relevant information may be retained for a limited period for security, legal, operational, backup, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

After the applicable retention period, information may be deleted or anonymized.

We may retain certain information where required by law or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

10. Account Deletion

Users can request permanent deletion of their Task Alert account, profile, and all associated personal data at any time.

You can request account deletion directly through the mobile app settings or by visiting our public Account & Data Deletion Request Page.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, we may take appropriate steps to delete the information.

Parents or legal guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information to us may contact us through the available support channel.

13. Cookies and Tracking

Task Alert's core task-management functionality does not require us to sell personal information or task content for advertising purposes.

If analytics, cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain their use where required by applicable law.

14. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, which may include the right to:
